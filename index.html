How it works

From a busy group to one page you can trust.

The group, read carefully

Several times a day, a name-free copy of the placement group is checked for new questions and answers.

Only official answers count

An answer counts only if it comes from the CAC or CAS. Other students' replies are left out.

Checked against the portal

Job deadlines, stages and must-dos come from the placement portal, which is checked every hour.

One page for you

The daily brief, companies and jobs, and a searchable FAQ, all in one place.

Daily brief

Deadline and JD changes, announcements, today's answers and open questions. At most 8 items a day, with a dot on what's new since you last looked.

Companies & jobs

Open roles, soonest deadline first, with a countdown that turns orange under 24 hours and red under 12. Stages, must-dos and each firm's Q&As sit in one card.

FAQ

The Placement Policy and general process answers, searchable in plain English: counters, clusters, offers, resumes, PPT rules.

Coming up

Every deadline in one list: applications, expected shortlists, PPTs and tests.

What it won't do

The rules that keep it trustworthy.

  • ✕
    No guessingIf the CAC or CAS hasn't answered, it says "Not answered yet. Ask in the group."
  • ✕
    No namesQuestions are anonymous and committee members aren't put in the spotlight. A changed answer shows only "changed by <first name>".
  • ✕
    No pay figuresCompensation questions point you to the placement portal.
  • ✕
    No "most asked" rankingsEvery question matters equally, so there are no counts per question.
  • ✕
    Not officialIt's a student project. The CAC notice board and CAS always win.
  • ✕
    No replacing the groupThe group stays where you ask. This is where you find what was already answered.
